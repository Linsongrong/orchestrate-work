# Routing Policy

This is the sole authority for assurance, minimum-sufficient work, route selection, ownership, evidence, side effects, recovery, and phase transition. The controller records the applicable decisions in the unified dispatch record in [task-contract.md](task-contract.md).

## Assurance And Minimum Work

Set one profile before dispatch. An explicit user selection wins. Otherwise use `prototype` only for an explicitly exploratory feasibility decision, proof of concept, spike, or disposable minimum experiment; use `formal` for durable, mergeable, complete, production-use, or ambiguous nontrivial work; use `release` only when release or readiness is explicit. "Quick", "MVP", and "try" alone do not lower durable implementation. A phase may be raised; lowering requires explicit user authorization and applies only to unstarted work.

| Profile | Required assurance | Avoid by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prototype` | One focused independent decision Gate per hypothesis experiment, after executor author checks | Separate Gates for subchanges, broad regression, full-history validation, or low-value sidecars |
| `formal` | Prototype risk floor, proportional independent verification of important artifacts, and final integrated verification | Reducing concrete safety, authority, or external-effect checks for speed |
| `release` | Formal assurance plus reproducible environment evidence, external-dependency review, and readiness checks | Calling an unverified environment or dependency release-ready |

For a prototype, the risk floor is only checks preventing a wrong experiment conclusion, authority/side-effect violation, irreversible harm, unreviewable result, or responsibility-boundary failure. Record hypothesis, minimum experiment, decision threshold, nonblocking backlog, and Gate/repair budget. Keep all tightly coupled design, adapters, mocks, implementation, and author checks in one executor-owned candidate. Add work, an agent, integration, or a check only when it changes an acceptance decision, protects a trust boundary or side effect, enables valid evidence reuse, or resolves cross-owner coordination. Each retained check needs a concrete, unique failure mode. Backlog speculative completeness; uncertainty is not a present requirement.

## Routes, Reuse, And Capacity

Choose task shape bottom-up as the lowest sufficient complete **model and reasoning effort**. Use Luna/medium for deterministic lookup or checks, Terra/medium for routine bounded implementation, diagnosis, or integration, and Sol/medium only for architecture, consequential conflict, adversarial verification, or an evidenced Terra gap. High effort requires its specific ambiguity, integration, or trust gap and evidence that the immediately lower effort is insufficient; above `high`, advance one level at a time with new such evidence. Above the Luna/medium baseline, state concrete lower-model and lower-effort insufficiency, not "complex", "important", or "high risk". Reassess task shape before every followup, after a material assumption or identity change, on verifier rejection, after two nonconverging repairs, at architecture-to-routine and implementation-to-installation/mechanical transitions, and after recovery. Keep the current route if it remains sufficient.

Every dispatch explicitly contracts model and effort, including an intentional same-as-controller choice. For fresh creation, use `<role>_<work>_<model>_<effort>` as an observability hint and record requested route until actual route is known; when route overrides are available, use `fork_turns="none"` and compact task-local context. Followup cannot reroute a reused agent. Reuse only when the same role, functional lineage, ownership, authority, budget, and **exact recommended route** remain and batching is worthwhile. A mismatch, including mandatory task-shape downgrade, requires a fresh suitable agent. Same-executor continuity never preserves Sol for Terra/Luna-shaped work. Cache may favor a genuine same-task, same-route reuse but never assumes cross-agent/model hits, causes Sol stickiness or fragmentation, or bypasses a distinct verifier. If the recommended route is unavailable, record an explicit fresh-agent fallback and its confidence effect.

When `sol` is selected for a prototype, record a bounded high-tier work budget and reassess at its boundary. Continue only while the same task shape and concrete Terra gap remain; renewal requires a fresh bottom-up decision. Paid model calls are budgeted side effects under the authority rule below.

Dispatch ready, disjoint work in a wave within capacity; preserve serial dependencies and reserve verification capacity where useful. One direct child layer only: no nested orchestration. Use direct child agents by default; suggest a user-owned task only for long-lived independently steerable work or strong worktree isolation, and only with explicit user authorization.

## Ownership, Evidence, And Side Effects

Give every agent exclusive ownership and task-local context. Executor owns its candidate and targeted author checks; verifier is read-only and returns an immutable verdict; integrator owns substantive integration and its checks; controller records and judges, but cannot implement and accept the same substantive artifact. Freeze candidate paths during independent verification. An integrator may modify only assigned integration paths; its output needs separate verification.

Assign check ownership before dispatch. Before evidence reuse, cross-owner/concurrent/mutable-state handoff, or independent verification, confirm the stable candidate identity or digest and required receipt. Reuse evidence only when identity, command, environment, result, and owner still match. Never transfer evidence from a superseded identity. Ordinary local low-risk work may use declared artifacts and targeted author evidence without a digest. A verifier starts only on a complete candidate, applicable verified handoff/identity, current author evidence, and no unresolved ordinary author-debugging issue; partial is never Gate-ready. A handoff mismatch is incomplete, not a quality Gate.

Authorize and account for costly, mutable, irreversible, sensitive, or external side effects before dispatch. State the actual authority and a named budget; `none` means none is allowed or needed. Keep secrets only in approved ephemeral channels unless the user authorizes persistence. Before consuming a budget, confirm remaining allowance. A pre-action validation failure consumes none; an attempted action with unknown outcome consumes one until reconciled. Every retry consumes the named budget; never retry silently. Stop retries on budget exhaustion, no new evidence, or nonconvergence, without a universal numeric retry limit. Exhaustion blocks only that side effect and retries, not otherwise-ready zero-consumption work. Mutable remote state needs an observed-state receipt, freshness bound, and reread trigger when it can change before dependent mutation or acceptance.

## Monitor, Recover, And Advance

Use completion gates and soft timeouts proportional to expected work and tool latency, never a universal minute limit. At timeout request one compact progress report; continue healthy, in-scope work producing new evidence. Interrupt or reroute only for stall, repeated failure, scope drift, noncritical hardening consuming the main line, or critical-path block without useful progress. Use adaptive time or decision heartbeats for long-running phases; synthesize objective, decision evidence, convergence/budget, next decision, and needed user action rather than forwarding raw worker progress.

First verifier failure returns to the original executor only when its current route still matches the recommendation; otherwise use a fresh executor and explicitly transfer repair ownership and functional base. In prototype, permit one narrow repair and re-Gate for the same hypothesis; another rejection requires reassessment and proceeds only with new evidence, acceptance criticality, and credible convergence. In formal/release, continue acceptance-critical repair while iterations produce new evidence and converge. Repeated failure, no new evidence, noncritical hardening, or two nonconverging rounds requires reassessing contract, assumptions, and approach before any model/effort escalation. Then backlog, redesign, reroute, or request authority as appropriate.

Preserve accepted artifacts and recover ordinary command, tool-path, JSON/reference, transient restart/socket/readiness, and healthy-observation-timeout faults within side-effect, retry, and convergence limits. Those faults do not justify partial. Stop a route after reassessment if it still fails; use another in-scope route or report a concrete blocker/request user decision. Admit discovered work only when acceptance-critical, blocking, or assumption-disproving; otherwise backlog and ask before material scope change. Emergency controller takeover is exceptional, recorded, and independently verified.

After acceptance, record completed deliverables, non-goals, latest verdict, next objective, profile, and remaining/new authority. Record a compact final route audit for each substantive task: task, role, requested/actual route, reused/new, and reason, to detect silent inheritance or one-model drift. Start the next phase only when already authorized; otherwise ask the user. A Gate not yet recorded is `pending integration`, not completion. On user-direction change, assess impact, stop only affected branches, revise dispatch records, and reject stale results.
